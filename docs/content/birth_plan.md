## Who will be with you `plan-with-me`

The people you'd like beside you, and anyone you'd rather wait outside.

## What helps you feel calm `plan-calm`

Music, prayer or chanting, soft light, moving around, a hand to hold: whatever settles you.

## Pain relief to talk over `plan-pain-relief`

What you'd like to know about the options, and anything you already feel strongly about. Your doctor will go through them with you.

## Just after the birth `plan-after-birth`

Holding your baby close, photos, quiet time together, and when you'd like visitors.

## Feeding wishes `plan-feeding`

How you hope to feed your baby, and any help you'd like with it.

## Anything else `plan-anything-else`

Customs, prayers, food from home, or anything you'd like your doctor and family to know.
